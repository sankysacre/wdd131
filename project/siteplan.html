Apex Coffee Crafters

WDD 131 - Website Planning Document

Prepared by: Senga Sanky Sacre

Site Name

Proposed Name: Apex Coffee Crafters

Why this name? "Apex" represents top-tier quality in coffee roasting, while "Crafters" emphasizes the artisanal skill involved in roast profiling, pour-over techniques, and bean selection.

Domain Idea: apexcoffeecrafters.org

Site Purpose

Apex Coffee Crafters will act as an interactive resource for coffee enthusiasts. The site will feature specialty coffee roast profiles, a filterable bean catalog, and a practical brew ratio calculator to help home baristas dial in their daily coffee.

Target Audience Scenarios

Common questions from site visitors:

  • "I have 25 grams of coffee beans. How many grams of water do I need for a V60 pour-over?"
  • "Which light roast single-origin coffees have fruity or floral tasting notes?"

Color Scheme

This planning document uses the exact color palette chosen for the project website:

Espresso Dark #2C1D11
Warm Amber #C87D55
Crema Gold #E8B87D
Oat Cream #FDFBF7
  • Espresso Dark (#2C1D11): Primary header background, titles, and main text.
  • Warm Amber (#C87D55): Subheadings, borders, and button accents.
  • Crema Gold (#E8B87D): Interactive element hover states and secondary highlights.
  • Oat Cream (#FDFBF7): Main page background color and section card containers.

Typography

The site uses a clean, modern font combination loaded from Google Fonts:

Heading Font: Montserrat (700 Bold / 600 Semi-Bold)
Body Font: Open Sans (400 Regular) — Used for body copy, bullet points, and navigation labels for clear readability across screens.
